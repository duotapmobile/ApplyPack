# Prospective service-error foundation

Status: local source implementation; v4 is inactive. Migration096 installs encrypted grouped reporting and owner triage. It does not enroll a purchase, activate the new terms, authorize a repair, or deliver files. Historical support continues under its recorded terms. The governing behavior is Amendment19 and `INTAKE_REVIEW_AND_SERVICE_ERROR_CORRECTIONS.md`.

The customer page `/my-applypack/material-lines/[id]/service-error` and its authenticated API accept only a line with an exact prospective contract binding. The existing delivery portal has not switched to this page. The future activation slice must switch legal content, checkout binding and customer support dispatch together after scoped repair and delivery are connected.

A report groups issues for one to three exact delivered file versions. Its stored reporting deadline is three calendar days after the original delivery in America/New_York, preserving the local time through daylight-saving changes. The database calculates that deadline. Reporting alone changes no files, payment, deadline, access expiry, approval or release. A reload shows the existing grouped receipt; an uncertain submission is reconciled by its exact request and content hash before an unchanged retry.

The private owner page `/admin/service-errors` requires fresh administrator MFA. Review includes the exact historical packet, original confirmed contact, candidate facts and parser/confirmation history, plus the delivered preview. Historical evidence never grants fresh rendering authority. The owner explicitly records a classification and affected versions. Accepted unfinished versions remain protected from retention cleanup and cannot disappear from a subsequent accepted decision. There is no automatic approval, regeneration or send action in this foundation.

Report text and owner explanations use purpose-bound encrypted `ap_sensitive_payloads`; the four new tables retain IDs, digests, classifications and immutable evidence relationships. Recovery includes those tables separately from the unchanged file-alias graph. Private reads reread ownership, active payloads and reviewer authority after decryption. Unknown writes preserve their payload and are not deleted or blindly repeated.

The required continuation is a separate repair obligation bound to the accepted decision and actually affected files. It must preserve the original paid line, deadlines, delivery history and unrelated documents, reconstruct truthful repair sources, render and review the changed artifacts, then require a separate private delivery decision. Only that complete workflow permits the prospective legal and checkout activation. This document is not hosted, provider, accessibility or production-launch evidence.
