# ApplyPack internal research basis

Internal decision memo, version 2026-09-04. This is not marketing copy and does not promise callback rates or universal causal effects. Product weights, confidence thresholds, diversity tolerance, and interface defaults are governed launch hypotheses requiring measurement, not research-proven hiring rules.

## Evidence-supported safeguards

- Confirm facts with the customer; never infer identity-sensitive traits or invent duties.
- Separate calendar duration, intensity, occupational capability, and confidence.
- Let customers recognize and confirm evidence instead of relying only on self-promotional free text.
- Treat employment-gap presentation as a controlled, testable choice with human review.
- Keep references separate, permissioned, purpose-scoped, revocable, and outside model inputs.
- Preserve uncertainty and source provenance rather than converting hypotheses into hard gates.

## Governed hypotheses requiring measurement

- Fit-component weights and diversity tolerance.
- Confidence thresholds and review escalation thresholds.
- Interface defaults, chip ordering, and recommendation wording.
- Whether a chronology/presentation alternative helps a particular population or role family.
- Job-source freshness, breadth, and ranking effects.

Measurement must use consented, privacy-safe, aggregate evidence; must not turn protected traits into targeting/scoring features; and must not publish callback percentages as guarantees.
