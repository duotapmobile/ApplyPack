# ApplyPack September 9 Final Product Amendment

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This amendment is the current product authority. It supersedes every earlier statement that ApplyPack has no subscription, that the $8 materials package is available only for a delivered Top 10 job, or that all job discovery must remain manual.

## Shared profile

Every product uses the same versioned four-step profile: identity and documents; desired and avoided work plus search breadth; customer review or correction of extracted facts and truthful additions; work, location, commute, compensation, dealbreakers, final review, and one Terms/Privacy acceptance. Conditional hidden answers do not affect matching. Private content is server-held, not placed in browser storage.

## Products

1. The subscription board costs $6.99 weekly, $19.99 monthly, or $44.99 every three calendar months. There is no free trial. It filters against confirmed profile evidence and uses neutral factual sorting, newest first by default. It never ranks, scores, labels a best match, or silently learns preferences from behavior. Board access, job details, counts, and application links require a server-verified paid-period entitlement.
2. The standalone $20 Top 10 requires no subscription. It delivers exactly 10 active, permitted, evidence-supported jobs within 24 hours, subject to capacity and the existing adjustment/refund contract. Its human review and comparative ranking remain private service operations.
3. The $8 package supplies one truthful tailored resume and one tailored cover letter for either a current eligible subscription-board job or a job in the customer's delivered Top 10. Human content and rendered-page approval are required before download. A purchased package and its order history remain accessible after subscription access ends.

## Source and review boundary

Automated collection is allowed only for a specifically documented and enabled source. A public endpoint, adapter, registry row, successful fixture, or configuration count is not by itself authorization for paid display. Collection must be bounded, resumable, idempotent, monitored, attributable, and freshness-controlled. Revoked, stale, unsafe, conflicted, or unauthorized records fail closed.

Human review is mandatory for ranked Top 10 delivery, generated documents, and policy-routed exceptions. ApplyPack must not claim that every subscription-board listing was individually human-reviewed.

## Release boundary

Staging uses Railway `staging` / `ApplyPack-staging`, a non-production Supabase environment, Stripe test mode, synthetic customer data, and allowlisted recipients. Synthetic listings may support interface testing only and must be clearly labeled. They do not satisfy the real-source launch gate. Production, `main`, `applypack.work`, live Stripe, production ingestion schedules, and production purchasing remain outside this amendment's deployment authorization.

All existing truthfulness, provenance, privacy, capacity, no-padding, accessibility, provider-verification, legal-approval, and production-approval gates remain in force.
