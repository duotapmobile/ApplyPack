## Homepage design principle

The homepage is not the place for long explanations. Each section must show a problem and ApplyPack's solution with a short headline, one or two supporting lines, and a visual simulation. Deeper explanations live on linked pages.

The visual simulations are demonstrations. They must not require the visitor to tap selections. They may animate once, then remain in a readable final state. The same meaning must exist as HTML text.

```text
ApplyPack reviews your prior work, education, side businesses, freelance work, volunteering, caregiving period, and other relevant experience.

We look for honest connections without pretending time outside traditional employment was something it was not.
```

### What if I want to leave my old career?

```text
That is one of the main reasons ApplyPack exists.

We do not search only by your old title.

We look at the work underneath it and identify different roles where the useful parts may still matter.
```

### What if I used to work in sales but never want to sell again?

```text
Tell us that sales is a dealbreaker.

We may still find relevant experience in account support, customer relationships, CRM systems, reporting, problem resolution, order coordination, and communication.

We keep the useful skills without sending you back to sales.
```

### Do I need an existing resume?

Recommended launch answer, pending user confirmation:

```text
Yes. Your resume may be old, outdated, poorly formatted, or written for another career.

A resume created entirely from scratch is a separate service and is not included in the $8 Apply Pack.
```

### Is a cover letter required?

```text
No.

An old cover letter can help us understand useful background or the way you naturally communicate, but it is optional.
```

### What does matched job mean?

```text
A matched job is a current opportunity selected around the criteria you approved and a reasonable overlap with your actual experience.

A match does not guarantee that an employer will consider you qualified or invite you to interview.
```

```text
For a Job Match Search, the clock begins after ApplyPack receives your completed and approved intake and successful payment.

For an Apply Pack, the clock begins after you select the job, confirm any updates, and complete payment.

Your confirmation page and email show the exact delivery deadline.
```

```text
Employers can close, pause, or change job listings at any time.
```
