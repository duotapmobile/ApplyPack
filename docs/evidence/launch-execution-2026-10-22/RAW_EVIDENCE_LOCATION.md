# Historical raw evidence retention

The reviewed Markdown summaries and supporting scripts in this folder are published with the launch source. Raw historical logs, JSON reports, text snapshots, proposed patches and browser captures remain preserved at their original paths in the canonical local workspace and are excluded from source publication. References to those local artifacts in the historical summaries describe local evidence; they are not GitHub artifact download links.

Before this launch implementation, all 688 existing changed/untracked files were copied and SHA-256 indexed under the ignored `evidence/paid-launch-2026-10-23/before` directory with `baseline.json`. Publication packaging also records the preserved raw artifact paths and hashes under that ignored evidence directory. Nothing in this packaging step deletes, edits or substitutes the original raw evidence.

New candidate and provider evidence is collected outside the frozen source, under ignored private evidence storage. The final committed-SHA GitHub workflow artifacts are the publication checks; historical local reports do not establish current production readiness.
